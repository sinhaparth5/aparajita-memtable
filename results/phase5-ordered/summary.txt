== arena cost and flush cost, 64 MiB write buffer, 1 thread ==
Flushes are how a rep's arena per key becomes visible: fewer keys per
memtable means more flushes for the same keyspace.

         rep   fill us/op   L0 files   keys/flush   flush us/key
   aparajita        1.031          7       285714          0.425
   skip_list        1.313          5       400000          0.441

  arena per key, Aparajita vs skiplist:  1.40x
  L0 files for the same 2M keys:        1.40x
  flush cost per key flushed:           0.96x

  The extra files are the arena overhead made visible. They do not
  cost extra flush time -- the same keys are written either way, at a
  per-key cost that is within a few percent -- but they are more L0
  files for compaction to merge, which this configuration disables and
  therefore does not measure.

Seek is the ordered workload the design is justified by and Phase 4 did
not measure. Flush is the other half of the same question: the iterator
that ordered iteration needs is also what a flush walks.
